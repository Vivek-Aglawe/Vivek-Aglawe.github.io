FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Is Cloudentra live yet?

Not fully. We are at an early stage and building the resume check first. Job listings come next. Book a demo and we will contact you.

Who is it for?

Freshers looking for a first job or internship, and experienced professionals changing roles. Colleges and recruiters can also ask for a demo.

Are the scores from a real employer ATS?

No. Scores are AI estimates that guide your edits. Every company uses different software, so no tool can promise a result.

What resume formats can I use?

You will be able to upload a PDF or paste your resume text.

What happens to my resume?

Your resume is personal data. The prototype does not store resumes, and we will publish a clear privacy notice before launch.

Which jobs will you show?

We plan to bring together openings from across India and remote roles worldwide, with direct links to apply.

Do you apply to jobs for me?

No. You apply on the employer’s own site, so you stay in control of what is sent.

How much will it cost?

We have not decided pricing yet. People who book a demo will hear about it first.

Can I sign in now?

Not yet. Accounts open when we launch. Until then, book a demo and we will email you.
